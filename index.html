01 / Why we exist

AI literacy belongs to everyone.

We help CBU students move from curiosity to confident, responsible use through practical learning and a community that welcomes beginners.

AI is reshaping every industry—not only engineering and computer science. Yet most students have nowhere on campus to build that fluency unless they are in a technical field.

We bridge that gap with workshops, case studies, speakers, and hands-on projects designed around real applications.

Who it is for
Any CBU student, in any major
Experience needed
None—curiosity is enough
Membership
Free and open to all students
What we focus on
Practical and responsible AI use
